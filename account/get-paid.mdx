The **Get Paid** feature helps you connect a payment provider to start accepting payments for your events. Complete provider onboarding before selling paid tickets. The connected provider processes payments and controls payout eligibility and timing.


## Connect a Payment Provider

1. Log in to your **Ticket Spot** account, open **Account** in the top navigation, and select **Get Paid** from the dropdown menu.

2. Payment Settings opens on its **Payment** tab, allowing you to connect a payment provider — such as **Stripe**, **Square**, or **PayPal** to start receiving payments for your events.

3. Click **Connect Stripe** (or your preferred provider, such as **Square**, **PayPal**) and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the setup.

After setup, confirm the provider shows as connected and check a completed test purchase in both Ticket Spot and the provider dashboard. Review payout timing and any outstanding verification requirements in the provider account.

The controls shown after connection depend on the provider and its account status:

| Provider | Controls in Ticket Spot |
|---|---|
| **Stripe** | **View Account**, connected or **Setup Required** status, and payment-method switches for Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Ideal, Bancontact, P24, EPS, and Twint. Select **Save Changes** after editing payment methods. |
| **Square** | Connected-account status and **Select Location**. A location change saves when selected. |
| **PayPal** | PayPal checkout, credit/debit-card fields, and Pay Later switches. Keep at least one method enabled, then select **Save Changes**. Where supported, review account readiness and refresh its status. Card fields require PayPal approval. Older connections may offer **Upgrade PayPal connection**. |

**Disconnect** removes the selected provider connection. Marketplace sites offer Stripe onboarding. A payment-method switch does not guarantee that method is available to every buyer; the provider also determines eligibility.

## Choose the correct payment workflow

- Website ticket checkout uses the provider configured for that integration. Available methods depend on the provider and account.
- Shopify product purchases use the [Shopify integration](/plugins/connect-shopify); configure Shopify payments in Shopify.
- Walk-up sales use the separate [Ticket Spot POS and Square handoff](/on-site/box-office-pos), or its cash workflow.
- [Installment tickets](/event-management/installment-payments) require Stripe card checkout. Business includes manual collection; Business Plus and Platform add automatic collection and optional retry days in **Payment Settings → Installments**.

Subscription charges, processor fees, organizer-configured service fees, and marketplace commissions are separate. Review the checkout breakdown and actual provider settlement rather than assuming the ticket's face value is the net payout.
