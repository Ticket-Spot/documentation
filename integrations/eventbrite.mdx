title: "Eventbrite"
description: "Connect Eventbrite to Ticket Spot and review imported event information and connection status."
---


Organizers can easily import their existing **Eventbrite** events and tickets into **Ticket Spot** for display in your Ticket Spot listings. This integration lets you manage all your events in one place, without switching between multiple platforms.


## Prerequisites

Before setting up the integration, make sure you have:

- ✅ An active **[Ticket Spot](https://ticketspotapp.com/)** account with access to the **Integrations** page.
- ✅ An active **[Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/blogquick-start-guide/)** organizer account to authorize and import your events.


## Integration

**Step 1:** Log in to your **Ticket Spot** account and click on the **Integrations** tab in the sidebar to open the integrations page.


**Step 2:** Find the **Eventbrite** platform under the **Event Platforms** category and click on the **Connect** button to continue to the provider’s authorization page.


<Frame caption="Open Integrations and choose Connect on the Eventbrite card. External authorization is a separate step.">
  <img src="../assets/refresh/integrations/integration-eventbrite-setup-card.png" alt="Open Integrations and choose Connect on the Eventbrite card. External authorization is a separate step." style={{ maxWidth: "440px" }} />
</Frame>

**Step 3:** Continue on the provider’s authorization page. The current connection flow redirects directly from the integration card.


**Step 4:** Authenticate the connection to your **Eventbrite** account by entering your sign-in credentials.

![sign-in](../assets/integrations/eventbrite/sign-in.png)


**Step 5:** Review the required permissions to understand what **Ticket Spot** can access and manage in your **Eventbrite** account. Then, click **Allow** to finish connecting your account.

![allow-button](../assets/integrations/eventbrite/allow-button.png)


**Step 6:** Once the integration is successfully connected, a **green check mark** will appear. This confirms that the **Eventbrite integration** is active in **Ticket Spot**.

![green-status](../assets/integrations/eventbrite/green-status.png)


## View Details

Choose **View Details** on an unconnected card, or from the **three-dot menu** on a connected card, to read what the integration supports. Check the card's connected-account label to confirm which account is linked.


## Disconnect

Remove the existing connection between your **Eventbrite** account and **Ticket Spot**.  
Once disconnected, **Ticket Spot** will no longer be connected to the **Eventbrite** platform.

Click the **three-dot menu** icon on the **Eventbrite integration** tile and select **Disconnect** from the dropdown menu.

![disconnect](../assets/integrations/eventbrite/disconnect.png)


Your **Eventbrite integration** will be successfully disconnected from **Ticket Spot**.
