title: "Set up your brand colors and logo"
description: "Configure your brand first so Ticket Spot widgets and attendee emails use your colors and logo on every plan."
---

Set up your brand before customizing individual widgets, emails, or event pages. **Basic brand identity is available on all plans**, including Free. Your shared logo and colors establish the starting appearance for your Ticket Spot experience.

## Navigation

1. Select the site you want to brand.
2. Click **Design** in the top navigation.
3. Select **Brand** (also labeled **Brand Design**).

## Brand Identity

| Setting | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Site Logo** | Upload your logo. Use Change Image to replace it and review it against your chosen background. |
| **Brand Primary Color** | Choose your primary brand color for supported buttons, accents, and backgrounds. Confirm the color selection and wait for the update to finish. |
| **Brand Text Color** | Choose a readable text color that works against the primary color. Confirm the selection and wait for completion. |
| **Brand Font** | Configure the shared font used by supported widget styles. Use the font controls to change, upload, or remove a custom font. |

Brand changes apply to the selected site. Check the site selector before changing a shared brand used by several events.

## Where your brand appears

| Surface | What to review |
|---|---|
| **Widget** | Shared colors and font; review individual widget overrides too. |
| **Event Page** | Logo and supported brand styles; use Event Page settings for its detailed presentation. |
| **Email** | Logo and colors on attendee email templates. |
| **Apple Wallet** | Logo and colors on the pass. |
| **PDF tickets** | Logo plus the ticket's own design settings. Use Tickets & Passes to customize its colors and layout. |

Use the **Widget**, **Event Page**, **Email**, and **Apple Wallet** preview choices to inspect your brand. Review readable text, buttons, and logo contrast rather than assuming every surface inherits every setting.

## Remove Ticket Spot Branding

**Remove Ticket Spot Branding** is a separate Business+ feature. Enable the removal setting when your plan includes it. It removes Ticket Spot social icons, the copyright line, and the Create Your Event link from attendee email footers. Event/site pages, printed tickets, and Apple Wallet passes are unaffected by this switch. Setting your own colors on Free does not also enable branding removal or custom domains.

## Save and check

Brand colors save when confirmed; wait for the successful update message. Refresh the selected preview if needed. After completing your design, open your published widget and make a test registration to inspect the actual email and ticket.
