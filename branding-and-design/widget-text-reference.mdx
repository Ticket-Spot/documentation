---
title: "Widget and checkout text reference"
description: "Find every customizable label and message for events, checkout, seating, membership verification, required tickets, and Shopify."
---

Open **Design → Widget → All Settings → Text**, then choose the section shown below. **Search settings** also finds individual labels. Wording changes do not enable a feature or change its purchase rules.

Preserve placeholders exactly as shown in each field's help. For example, keep `{ticket}`, `{quantity}`, `{count}`, `{step}`, `{completed}`, `{total}`, `{remaining}`, `{price}`, and `{duration}` in messages that need those values. Installment fields may use double braces such as `{{amount}}` and `{{count}}`. These examples are shown as placeholders in the reference tables below.

**Automatic translation** changes supported event text to the visitor's language; the search-filter translation control depends on it. Outgoing emails and tickets have separate [Multilingual communications](./multilingual-communications) settings. Shopify variant labels apply to the Shopify product experience.

New checkout wording includes per-attendee progress, copy-from-purchaser and save/next actions, review/order errors, waitlist details, required-ticket guidance, seat-limit/unavailable messages, add-on quantities and conflicts, and membership verification/pricing. Test the corresponding states after changing their wording; a normal purchase will not exercise error and sold-out messages.
