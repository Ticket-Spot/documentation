Use this reference alongside the [Design setup guide](./design-overview). Open **Design**, select the named sidebar area, and choose the ticket or communication type where applicable. Availability varies by plan; basic Brand identity remains available on all plans.

Text and label fields change attendee-facing wording. Show/Hide controls change visibility. Color controls change the named element’s appearance. They do not change the underlying ticket, membership, payment, or email-sending rules. Most design controls save automatically; wait for the update to finish and verify the actual attendee experience.

## Brand and Widget

Start with [Brand](./brand-design): Site Logo, Brand Primary Color, Brand Text Color, and Brand Font. **Remove Ticket Spot Branding** controls the email footer separately on Business+.

Under **Widget**, choose **Design**, **Display**, **Layout**, **Event Filters**, **Visitor search & filters**, **Checkout**, or **Text**. The widget Design category is hidden for Wix sites. See [Widget customization](./widget-customization-overview) and its linked field guides. Choosing a new widget theme replaces its existing color/font overrides; review it before changing a customized widget.

## Ticket PDF — custom designer

Switch from **Basic** to the custom designer to arrange the ticket. Canvas settings include **Width (px)**, **Height (px)**, **Enable Custom Design**, and **Show Grid**. Text settings include **Content**, **Font Size**, **Font Family**, **Font Weight**, **Text Color**, **Text Alignment**, **Max Width**, **Background Color**, **Padding**, and **Border Radius**. Use the token tools to insert event and attendee data. Test a long name and an actual QR scan after changing the layout.

## Attendee Ticket Page

The six controls are **Allow Attendee Edits**, **Show Order Details Section**, **Show Tickets Section**, **Show Documents Section**, **Show Cancel or Transfer Action**, and **Show Contact Host Action**. Follow [the attendee ticket-page guide](./attendee-ticket-page) for their effects and immediate-save behavior.
