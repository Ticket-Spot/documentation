---
title: "Desktop and mobile widget layouts"
description: "Choose independent desktop, mobile, and date/time layouts and configure calendar display."
---

Open **Design → Widget → All Settings → Layout**. Select **Desktop**, **Mobile**, or **Date & time** first.

1. Under Desktop, choose **List & cards** or **Grid**, then choose a layout thumbnail.
2. Under Mobile, choose a layout for phones independently of desktop.
3. Under Date & time, choose **Daily View**, **Full Calendar**, or **Time Slot Picker** for time-based event browsing.
4. For the **Calendar** desktop layout, configure its initial view, week start, visible hours, next-day threshold, Today button, weekends, and time visibility.
5. Wait for saving and preview all applicable screen sizes and event types.

The [layout reference](./widget-layout-reference) names every available layout and calendar control. **Calendar**, **Calendar View**, and **Full Calendar** are distinct choices. The calendar control labeled **Time format** selects Month, Week, Day, or List; it is not the listing's 12/24-hour time format.

Test long titles, portrait and landscape images, several dates, empty results, and a phone-sized checkout. Calendar display hours do not change an event's actual date/time or ticket sales window.
