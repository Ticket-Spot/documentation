## Create the right ticket offer

| Your goal | Practical guide |
| --- | --- |
| Set up a class, tour, concert, festival, or another format | [Choose your event type](/event-types/overview) |
| Offer dates, time slots, or recurring sessions | [Timed entry, recurring classes, and passes](/event-management/timed-entry-and-passes) |
| Let one booking reserve an entire time slot | [Exclusive bookings](/bookings/exclusive-time-slots) |
| Set prices, quantities, and shared capacity | [Ticket setup](/event-management/ticket-setup) and [capacity rules](/event-management/ticket-capacity) |
| Sell reserved seats, tables, or booths | [Seating charts](/seating-charts/create-and-assign) and [advanced layouts](/seating-charts/advanced-layouts) |
| Sell class packages or annual passes | [Memberships](/memberships/overview) |
| Offer extras, a waitlist, or installment payments | [Add-ons](/event-management/event-add-ons), [waitlists](/event-management/waitlist), or [installments](/event-management/installment-payments) |

Selling through Shopify? Start with the [Shopify feature hub](/plugins/shopify-overview) for the storefront steps that apply to these features.

## Shape the booking experience

Set your [brand](/branding-and-design/brand-design), choose a [widget layout](/branding-and-design/layout), and collect the [registration details](/event-management/additional-information) or [waivers](/event-management/waivers) you need. The [design setup guide](/branding-and-design/design-overview) connects the website, tickets, emails, and attendee pages. Use [language and locale settings](/branding-and-design/language-and-locale) for a multilingual audience.

## Manage orders and attendees

Use the [attendee dashboard](/attendee-management/managing-attendees) to find a booking. From there, [review an order](/attendee-management/managing-orders), [refund or cancel](/attendee-management/refunds-and-cancellations), [transfer tickets](/attendee-management/transfers-and-ticket-changes), or [check ticket delivery](/attendee-management/email-and-sms-delivery).

## Run your event

Follow [the event-day guide](/guides/run-your-event) to prepare staff, mobile scanning, offline check-in, box-office sales, printed tickets, or a self check-in kiosk.

## Promote and measure

Send [event emails](/event-management/email-communication) and [SMS](/event-management/sms-communication), create [campaign tracking links](/event-management/tracking-links), and use [analytics and reports](/analytics/analytics-overview) to understand sales and attendance. Connect your [marketing, calendar, or event tools](/integrations/integrations-overview) when you need them.

## Manage your account and team

Check [billing and usage](/account/billing-and-plans), [payment settings](/account/get-paid), [staff roles](/team/invite-team-roles), and [workspaces](/site-settings/add-and-switch-sites). Multi-organizer operations have a separate [marketplace guide](/site-settings/marketplace-and-organizations).
