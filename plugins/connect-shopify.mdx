Connecting **Ticket Spot** to your **Shopify** store makes it easy to publish events as products and sell tickets directly through Shopify Checkout. Once connected, you can send any Ticket Spot event to Shopify, add the Ticket Selector to your product page, and allow guests to receive their tickets immediately after purchase.

This guide shows how to install the app and enable optional Shopify features so you can start selling tickets on Shopify quickly and easily.

## Navigation

**Step 1:** Click on the site name in the top-left corner, open the dropdown menu, and select **+ Add New Site** to start adding a new site.

![click-site](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/click-site.png)

A modal window will appear where you can **Create or Connect Your Site**.

![create-connect-site-modal](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/create-connect-site-modal.png)


**Step 2:** Click **Connect Shopify** to start linking your store.

![click-conenct-shopify-btn](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/click-conenct-shopify-btn.png)


## Install the Ticket Spot App

1. You’ll be redirected to the Ticket Spot page in the **Shopify** App Store. Click **Install** to proceed.

![click-install-btn1](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/click-install-btn1.png)


2. Shopify authorization screen will appear, where you can authenticate yourself using your store credentials to complete the connection.

![shopify-login](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/shopify-login.png)


Click **Install** to authorize Ticket Spot and grant access to view personal data and manage store data.

![click-install-btn2](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/click-install-btn2.png)


Your **Shopify** store is now **connected**, and you will be redirected back to the **Ticket Spot** web app, where you can create your first event.

![my-store-dashboard](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/my-store-dashboard.png)


## Create Your Event

To create your event, enter the basic details, such as the event name, date, time, and venue. These fields help set up your event in Ticket Spot and prepare it for publishing.

For a full step-by-step walkthrough, please refer to our guide: **[How to Create an Event with Ticket Spot](../event-management/create-event-overview)**.

![click-create-event](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/click-create-event.gif)


## Publish Your Event to Shopify

1. Click on the **Publish** button at the top of your event page.

![click-publish-btn](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/click-publish-btn.png)


2. Click on the **Publish to Shopify** to publish your event and updates to your Shopify store. This creates or updates the Shopify product that customers can purchase.

![publish-to-shopify-btn](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/publish-to-shopify-btn.png)


3. Click on the **Share** button to post your event on social media or copy the event link, allowing shoppers to view your event and purchase tickets directly.

![share-btn](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/share-btn.png)


## Check Out the Event

1. Click on the **Event Page** or open the event link to open your event page, where your event is listed.

![click-event-page-btn](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/click-event-page-btn.gif)


2. Click on **Buy Tickets** to purchase a ticket for the event and complete the checkout.

![click-buy-tickets-btn](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/click-buy-tickets-btn.gif)


## View Attendees in Your Dashboard

Once the purchase is completed, Shopify automatically sends attendee and order details to Ticket Spot.

Open your event in Ticket Spot and click on the **Manage Attendees** to view attendees of your event.

![manage-attendees-btn](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/manage-attendees-btn.gif)


From here, you can see the full attendee list, update their status, view their details, send tickets, and manage all attendee actions in one place.

![attendee-list](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/attendee-list.png)


## Configure the Ticket Selector for advanced ticketing

Use the **Ticket Selector** for seating charts, a calendar/date/time-slot selection experience, access codes, and ticket quantity controls. A standard Shopify variant dropdown does not provide the full Ticket Spot selection flow. Configure the event and its ticket rules in Ticket Spot first.

[Watch the Shopify setup video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=grq-oS9AFgI).

1. Publish or update the event's linked Shopify product from Ticket Spot.
2. Open your Shopify theme editor and select the **product template** used by that event product.
3. In the product section, add the **Ticket Selector** app block.
4. Choose **Display Mode → Embed inline** to show the selector on the page, or **Modal** for the modal experience.
5. Save the template and open the published event product page.
6. Test the configured seating chart, calendar/time slots, access codes, and quantity rules in the selector, then continue through Shopify checkout.

The Ticket Selector handles ticket selection on the product page; the Shopify product flow still checks out through Shopify. The **Thank You Page** ticket-access block is separate and should be configured below.

Older installations may use the **Event Controls / Ticket Spot Controls** app embed. Do not confuse that body-level embed with the **Ticket Selector** product app block. Use the current product block workflow for the advanced selection experience described here.

## Existing product configuration controls

Go back to your Ticket Spot event and click on the **Shopify Setup** button.

![go-shopify-setup1](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/go-shopify-setup1.gif)


In Shopify Setup, you can:

- **Select a Collection** — Organize your event by adding it to a specific collection so customers can easily find it.

- **Select a Location** — Select the Shopify inventory location used for the product. Configure the attendee-facing venue separately under Venue & Location.

![shopify-setup](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/shopify-setup.gif)


### Product Page Config:

- **Customize the “Buy Tickets” Button:** You can customize the Buy Tickets button text—for example, change it to **Checkout** or any label you prefer.

![enable-ticketspot-selector](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/product-page-config.gif)


- **Enable Ticket Spot Selector:** Controls the selector experience for installations using this product configuration. The selector changes ticket selection; the product flow continues through Shopify checkout.

![enable-ticketspot-selector](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/enable-ticketspot-selector.gif)


For a modal selector installation, the **Buy Tickets** button opens the selector. For the app block, the selected Display Mode determines whether selection is inline or modal. Test the product template you actually use before sharing it.

![ticketspot-selector-buy-ticket](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/ticketspot-selector-buy-ticket.gif)


## Enable the Ticket Block on the Thank You Page

Ticket Spot gives customers instant access to their tickets—QR code, PDF, and Apple Wallet—directly on Shopify’s Thank You page, making the post-checkout experience faster and more convenient.

1. In Shopify, go to **Settings** and select **Checkout**. Then click on **Customize** to open the Checkout editor.

![select-thankyou-page-save](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/thankyou-page1.gif)


2. In the **Apps** section, select the **Ticket Spot – Ticket Access** block.

![select-thankyou-page-save](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/app-embed-ticketspot.png)


3. Select the **Thank You Page** and click **Save** to apply the changes.

![select-thankyou-page-save](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/select-thankyou-page-save.gif)


## Access Tickets on the Thank You Page

1. Complete your **ticket purchase** through the **Shopify** checkout page.

![thankyou-page-display](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/checkout-thankyou-page.gif)


2. Once checkout is completed, the **Thank You** page will display your order details, including **View Tickets** and **Add to Wallet**.

![thankyou-page-display](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/thankyou-page-display.png)


3. Click **View Tickets** or **Add to Wallet** to instantly download and access your ticket.

![view-ticket-add-wallet](../assets/plugins/connect-shopify/view-ticket-add-wallet.gif)

These features streamline your entire ticketing flow—helping you sell more, simplifying checkout, and giving guests a smoother, more enjoyable experience.

## Verify advanced ticket controls

Test an available and sold-out seat, the correct event date/time, ticket minimum/maximum quantities, a hidden ticket with an access code, and any [Required Ticket](../event-management/ticket-setup#required-ticket) dependency you configured. Confirm that the selected event/ticket data survives checkout and appears in Ticket Spot's attendee records. Test ticket access on the thank-you page separately.
