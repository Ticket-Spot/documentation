Choose where your guests will book. Each guide covers installation, publishing, and a visitor test. After connecting, the shared [event and feature guides](/guides/overview) help you build out your event.

## Shopify

Start with [selling event tickets on Shopify](/plugins/connect-shopify). Ticket Spot manages your events and ticket selection; customers pay through Shopify checkout. The guide takes you through the linked product, any required Ticket Selector setup, and ticket delivery.

Already connected? The [Shopify guide hub](/plugins/shopify-overview) helps you find dates and time slots, seating, capacity, attendee details, add-ons, and troubleshooting.

## Squarespace and other websites

Use Ticket Spot's generated embed code to display an event calendar or ticket widget inside your website.

- **Squarespace:** follow the [Squarespace embed guide](/plugins/connect-squarespace).
- **Other website builders or custom websites:** use [the website embed guide](/plugins/embed-on-your-website). Your site must allow the required script and widget container.
- **Lovable:** use the [Lovable setup guide](/plugins/connect-lovable) to generate an integration prompt.

Need a public event link without adding a widget? Review the [hosted event page](/event-management/eventdetails) and its [plan availability](/account/feature-reference).

## After installation

1. [Create your first event](/getting-started/first-event), or open an event you already have.
2. Confirm the payment and publishing steps for your website.
3. Complete the [launch checklist](/getting-started/launch-checklist), including ticket delivery and the attendee record.

To import events or connect calendars, online meetings, and marketing tools, use [Connected apps](/integrations/integrations-overview).
