Sell event tickets as products on your Shopify store. Configure the event and its booking rules in Ticket Spot, let customers pay through **Shopify checkout**, and manage their tickets and attendance in Ticket Spot.

## Sell your first tickets

Start with the [Shopify quickstart](./connect-shopify). It takes you from app installation to a verified order and delivered ticket:

1. Connect your store and create the event.
2. Publish its Shopify product and configure the ticket-selection flow.
3. Set up ticket access, then complete a storefront purchase.
4. Verify the attendee record and confirmation before sharing the event.

For paid Shopify product purchases, configure payments in **Shopify**. Ticket Spot's **Get Paid** connections apply to other checkout routes; see [payment workflows](/account/get-paid#choose-the-correct-payment-workflow).

Already connected? Open the correct store in Ticket Spot and choose the task below.

## Set up your storefront

| What you want to do | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Let customers select tickets, dates, and seats on an event product | [Install the Ticket Selector and assign its product template](./shopify-ticket-selector#install-the-product-block). |
| Show an event listing or calendar on a store page | [Create a widget](/branding-and-design/widget-customization-overview#open-the-widget-editor), then use the Shopify **Event Widget** block with its widget ID. [Compare the Shopify blocks](./shopify-ticket-selector#choose-the-correct-shopify-block). |
| Collect each guest's name, questions, or waivers | [Configure attendee-information collection](./shopify-ticket-selector#product-settings-and-attendee-details). |
| Give customers ticket access immediately after payment | [Add Ticket Spot – Ticket Access to the Thank You page](./connect-shopify#enable-the-ticket-block-on-the-thank-you-page). |

The **Event Widget** lists events. The **Ticket Selector** handles booking on an event product. **Ticket Access** shows tickets after payment. Choose the block for the part of the customer journey you are setting up.
