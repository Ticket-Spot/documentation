Open **Settings → Mobile Check-in** to choose what happens after a ticket scan. The two automatic check-in controls require Business or higher.

**Step 1**: Log in to your Ticket Spot account and click on the **Settings** tab from the top navigation bar.

**Step 2**: Select **Mobile Check-in** from the left sidebar to open the check-in settings page.

**Step 3**: Set the two automatic check-in switches for your door workflow. They are separate switches.

| **Option** | **Description** | **Example** |
|-----------|------------------|-------------|
| **Auto Check-in Entire Order on Scan** | Automatically checks in eligible attendees associated with the scanned ticket’s order. | A family or group arrives together. One scan checks everyone in. |
| **Auto Check-in Individual Attendee on Scan** | Automatically checks in the scanned attendee. When off, staff must use the check-in button after scanning. | Helpful when you need to verify each attendee individually. |

**Step 4**: After choosing your check-in method, click the **Save** button in the top-right corner to apply your changes.

After saving, a confirmation message will appear to show that your check-in site settings have been successfully updated.

Keep order-level automatic check-in off when people in the same order may arrive separately. Before opening the doors, test your chosen settings with a demo order and confirm which attendee records change.

## Best Practices

- **Choose the right mode** based on how your attendees typically arrive (groups vs. individuals).
- **Test your check-in method** before the event using a sample ticket.
- **Train your staff** so they understand whether you’re using group check-in or individual check-in.
- **Use a device with a reliable camera** for faster and smoother scanning during entry.
