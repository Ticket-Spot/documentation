---
title: "View and manage membership holders"
description: "Find membership holders, inspect visit history and expiry, and add a member manually."
---

Use the Memberships area to manage the program itself and see who holds it.

## Find a membership

1. Open **Memberships** in the top navigation.
2. Filter by status if needed.
3. Review **Name**, **Price**, **Duration**, **Events**, **Inventory**, **Holders**, and **Status**.
4. Click **Edit** to change program settings and save with **Update Membership**.

The delete control soft-deletes the membership after confirmation. Review existing holders and event assignments before using it; deleting a program is not a refund workflow.

## View who is assigned

Click the active/total count in the **Holders** column to open that membership's members page. Use **Search members...** and the **Status** filter to find a person.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Member** | The holder's identity. |
| **Purchased** | Purchase date. |
| **Expires** | Membership expiry. |
| **Last Visit** | Most recent recorded visit. |
| **Visits** | Recorded visits and applicable limits. |
| **Status** | The holder's membership status. |

Select a member to open their detail panel. Review the available membership, visit, and notes information there.

## Add a holder manually

1. On the membership's members page, click **Add Member**.
2. In **Add Membership Holder**, enter the member's email address.
3. Add their first name, last name, phone, and internal notes as needed.
4. Click **Add Member** and wait for completion.
5. Find the member in the list and inspect their details.

This assigns a holder; it is not a card-payment checkout. Confirm any payment arrangement separately. Verify the holder's ticket/QR access before their first visit.

## Resend, extend, or cancel a membership

Open the holder's detail panel from the membership's members page.

- Use **Resend QR** when a member needs another copy of their membership access details. Confirm the recipient's email first.
- Use **Extend Membership** to extend the holder's access. Complete the extension controls and verify the updated expiry in the member record.
- Use **Cancel Membership** when the holder should no longer use the membership. Review the confirmation and check the resulting status.

An extension applies to the selected holder. Changing the program's standard duration is a separate edit. Cancellation does not itself describe a payment refund; handle any refund in the payment workflow and verify the result separately.
