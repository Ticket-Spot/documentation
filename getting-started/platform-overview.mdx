## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When do tickets count toward my monthly attendee allowance?">
    Tickets count in the calendar month they are issued, when the purchase or registration is processed. Event dates and check-in dates do not change that month. If you issue 600 tickets in October for November and December events, all 600 count toward October. On a 500-attendee Professional allowance, you need capacity for the extra 100, assuming no other October usage.

    See [monthly allowance rules and ticket packs](/account/billing-and-plans#when-tickets-count-toward-your-monthly-allowance).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see how many included attendees I have used and how many remain?">
    Select your site and open **Events**. Click the **Current plan** card's heading or usage message to open **Usage & Add-Ons**, then read **ATTENDEES → Used this cycle**, **Remaining**, and **Available Now**. Owners and admins can see this card, including when it only says **Usage is within plan limits**. **Manage plan** opens Billing to change your plan or add credits.

    <Frame caption="Click the Current plan heading or usage message to see the detailed balance. Demo account shown.">
      <img src="../assets/refresh/account/monthly-usage-entry.png" alt="Ticket Spot Demo Events dashboard with the Current plan usage message highlighted" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Frame>

    Follow the [monthly usage walkthrough](/account/billing-and-plans#check-your-current-monthly-usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Ticket Spot in another language?">
    Turn on **Automatic translation** in **Design → Widget → Quick Settings** for supported built-in widget and checkout labels. For translated emails and tickets, enable **Multilingual communications** separately in **Design → Attendee Communication**. Your own event descriptions and custom messages need their own translations.

    See the [Language and locale FAQ](/getting-started/language-and-locale-faq) for preview languages, custom wording, translated emails, and regional settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For broader questions about pricing, integrations, and event features, visit the [general Ticket Spot FAQ](https://ticketspotapp.com/faq). The [Language and locale FAQ](/getting-started/language-and-locale-faq) focuses on translation and regional settings.
