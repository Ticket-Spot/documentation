## Build the event you have in mind

- **Classes, tours, concerts, or festivals:** follow a guide for [your event type](/event-types/overview).
- **Dates, time slots, and capacity:** set up [recurring sessions and timed entry](/event-management/timed-entry-and-passes).
- **Reserved seats or memberships:** explore [seating charts](/seating-charts/create-and-assign) or [memberships and passes](/memberships/overview).
- **Getting ready for event day:** prepare [check-in and on-site sales](/guides/run-your-event).
